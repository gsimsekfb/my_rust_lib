### What These Roles Actually Do

Unlike Web3 roles (which focus on consensus mechanisms and crypto primitives), Non-Web3 Backend roles involve:

- **Building high-throughput backend services** for real-time risk, pricing, trading engines, and market making systems (C++ and Rust) 
- **Designing and maintaining core platform infrastructure** including data pipelines, search, and identity systems 
- **Creating scalable integrations** with critical financial systems like QuickBooks Online, NetSuite, and Microsoft Business Central 
- **Developing cloud-native systems** running at scale in AWS with infrastructure-as-code (AWS CDK) 
- **Building distributed systems** with messaging and low-latency persistence (Kafka, Redis, RabbitMQ) 

### Why This Sector Is Growing

1. **Volume of Roles**: Non-Web3 backend represents 49 backend roles out of 311 total remote dev jobs (~16% of all remote dev jobs) according to March 2026 data—this excludes DevOps, full-stack, and frontend counts . 2. **Skill Requirements**: These roles demand mastery of asynchronous programming, advanced concurrency patterns, thread safety, and lock-free data structures—skills that command premium compensation .

3. **Industry Spread**: Defense tech , financial services , enterprise SaaS , and IoT  are all competing for the same talent pool.

4. **Remote-First Culture**: Unlike trading firms, general backend roles at startups and mid-sized companies are overwhelmingly remote-friendly .
